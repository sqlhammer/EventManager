## Walkthrough (curl, hub running)
Prereq: an event with a division and a bracket generated by the hub from the cleared roster.
1. **Assign a device to a mat/division**: `POST /api/events/{eventId}/competition/mat-assignments {"deviceId":D,"divisionId":X}`.
2. **Start the division**: `POST /api/events/{eventId}/competition/divisions/{X}/bracket:start`. Further regeneration is now blocked.
3. **Advance a match**: `POST .../divisions/{X}/bracket:advance {"matchId":M,"winnerId":W,"method":"Points"}` → standings update.
4. **Standings**: `GET .../divisions/{X}/standings` → ordered by wins.
5. **Finalize**: `POST .../divisions/{X}:finalize` → placements by wins (US-601).
6. **Disputes**: `POST .../disputes {"divisionId":X,"matchId":M,"reason":"..."}` then `PUT .../disputes/{id} {"resolution":"..."}`.

**Mat authority** (US-406): a score from a device not assigned to the match's division is rejected — exercised by the `Foreign_mat_score_is_rejected` test (scoring intake path). Bracket outcomes are computed by the U1 `ScoringEngine`; advancement/finalization by the U1 `BracketEngine`.

## Deferred
Live-standings push to spokes uses the U4a `IHubPush` seam (SignalR concrete deferred); check-in status-board population wires with U6; bracket-regeneration-on-move (US-309) emits `DivisionMoved` and the caller re-invokes generation with the updated field.
